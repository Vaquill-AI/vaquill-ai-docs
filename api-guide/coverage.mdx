**6.0M+ distinct sections, indexed as 21M+ retrieval passages**, across federal law and all 56 states and territories.

The corpus grows continuously, so this page gives magnitudes and the table below gives the measured split.
For a number you can put in a contract, call the endpoint.
It is free, self-describing and authoritative over anything written here by hand.

```bash
# The live, self-describing answer. Free, and authoritative over this page.
curl https://api.vaquill.ai/api/v1/us/statutes/coverage \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer vq_key_..."
```

<Note>
  Counts are **distinct sections**, one per provision or document, matching what `GET /us/statutes/coverage` returns.
  Longer documents split into several passages for retrieval, so the index holds more passages than sections.
  A citation lookup still resolves to one section with its full text.
  Past editions held for point-in-time lookups are counted too, and `statusBreakdown.superseded` gives that share per corpus.
</Note>

## What's in the corpus

Counts measured **2026-11-07**.

| Corpus | Jurisdictions | Sections | `corpusType` | Refresh |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| State statutes | 56 | 2,642,636 | `STATE` | Monthly, quarterly for AR, GA, TN and VI |
| State regulations | 56 | 1,504,702 | `REGULATION` | Monthly where the publisher supports one, per state |
| State court rules | 56 | 69,931 | `STATE_RULES` | Monthly |
| State constitutions | 53 | 13,057 | `STATE_CONSTITUTION` | Monthly |
| State registers (notices, not codified law) | 24 | 153,850 | `STATE_REGISTER` | Not stated |
| State agency guidance | 49 | 13,402 | `STATE_AGENCY_GUIDANCE` | Monthly, per state, in rotation |
| State AG opinions | 45 | 109,438 | `STATE_AG_OPINION` | Monthly, per jurisdiction, new opinions only |
| Federal, 16 corpora | Federal | 1,534,416 | See [Federal](/api-guide/coverage/federal) and [Treaties](/api-guide/coverage/treaties) | Daily to weekly, on amendment for the Constitution |
| [State session laws](/api-guide/session-laws) | 52 | Live on the endpoint | Own endpoints | Not stated |

The 56 jurisdictions are the 50 states, DC, Puerto Rico, Guam, the Northern Mariana Islands, the US Virgin Islands and American Samoa.
All 56 carry statutes, regulations and court rules, and 53 carry constitutions.
Guam, the Virgin Islands and DC have no constitution of their own, and the organic acts of Guam and the Virgin Islands are federal law.
Session laws cover the 50 states, DC and Puerto Rico from 1975, and the count held per jurisdiction and session changes as collection proceeds, so the live counts are on `GET /us/statutes/coverage`.

Every section is indexed for hybrid semantic and keyword search, and mirrored in multiple formats (HTML, PDF, XML, plain text, DOCX) where the source provides them.

## Federal

Federal law is split into 16 corpus types, from the US Code, the CFR and the Federal Register to agency guidance, adjudications, the Federal Rules and treaties.
The [federal table](/api-guide/coverage/federal) lists every one with its scope limits, and [treaty coverage](/api-guide/coverage/treaties) covers the two treaty tokens.
Congressional bill text of the 119th Congress is held as the opt-in `FEDERAL_BILL` token, outside every total; it is described on the federal page and the [Roadmap](/api-guide/roadmap).

Agency guidance is the one corpus with a registry behind it: **53 named sources across 26 agencies** behind the single `corpusType=AGENCY_GUIDANCE` token, each narrowable with `source`.
See the [source registry](/api-guide/coverage/agency-guidance).

## State corpora

Per-jurisdiction counts for all 56 are in the [matrix](/api-guide/coverage/states).
A few sets, mainly professional-conduct and bar rules and all of American Samoa's Code, Constitution and court rules, are read from a bar association's site, and the states page [says which](/api-guide/coverage/states#where-bar-associations-supply-the-text).

### State administrative registers

`corpusType=STATE_REGISTER` holds the notices a state printed in its official register: proposed, adopted and emergency rule text, one document per item, cited by register volume and page.
It is not codified law, it is never ranked as primary law, and it is served with `goodLawStatus: "unknown"`.
See [per-jurisdiction counts](/api-guide/coverage/states#state-administrative-registers).

### State agency guidance

State-issued regulatory guidance that interprets state law, almost all of it insurance-regulator bulletins.
Queryable with `corpusType=STATE_AGENCY_GUIDANCE` paired with `state`.
It is not a full rollout: it lands state by state, and some states may never be built.
See [per-state sources and counts](/api-guide/coverage/states#state-agency-guidance).

### State attorney general opinions

Formal written opinions of a state Attorney General, construing that state's own statutes, constitution and regulations for an officer, legislator or local government who asked.
Queryable with `corpusType=STATE_AG_OPINION` paired with `state`.
The rollout is by jurisdiction: 45 of the 52 jurisdictions surveyed are live, and the survey measured roughly 184,600 individually addressable opinions, so what is live is a fraction of what exists.

<Warning>
  **AG opinions are persuasive authority, not binding law.**
  An AG opinion is often the only interpretive authority for a provision no court has reached.

  <GoodLawUnknown />

  See [Status and currency](/api-guide/concepts/status-and-currency).
</Warning>

Per-state ranges, scope limits and notes (Delaware, Montana, Arkansas, Indiana, Florida and others) are on [state coverage](/api-guide/coverage/states#state-attorney-general-opinions).

## Refresh cadence

The Refresh column of the table above says how often each corpus is re-pulled from its official source.
State statutes refresh monthly, except Arkansas, Georgia, Tennessee and the US Virgin Islands, which refresh quarterly.
For the age of any single source, call [`GET /boards`](/api-guide/alerts).
It is free and carries `lastRetrievedAt` and `cadence` per source.
`GET /us/statutes/coverage` reports what each corpus is *current through* in the publisher's own words, which is a different question and is not available for every state.

### Which statute cadence applies where

Compare `lastRetrievedAt` against `cadence` on `GET /boards` to see how recently a source was pulled.
The sections stay queryable at the `currentThrough` the coverage response reports.

<Accordion title="Cadence details">

**Monthly, 46 jurisdictions.**
These state statute boards report `cadence: "monthly"` on `GET /boards`.

**Quarterly, 4 jurisdictions.**
Arkansas, Georgia, Tennessee and the US Virgin Islands are pulled on a quarterly slot, tracking legislatures that sit for part of the year.
They report `cadence: "quarterly"`.

Read the `cadence` field on `/us/statutes/coverage` and `/boards` as the declared staleness window a board is alerted against, not as a ceiling on how often the corpus is re-pulled.

**Five jurisdictions are covered but not watchable**: Delaware, Hawaii, Mississippi, New Mexico and Utah.
Their statutes are live, complete and queryable.
What is held back is the automated re-pull, so they are absent from `GET /boards` rather than listed as idle.
A board is switched on only once its refresh has been shown to work, so `GET /boards` lists what can be watched, never what is covered.
For these five, read currency from `GET /us/statutes/coverage`.

State AG opinions are issued once and not revised, so a refresh looks for new opinions rather than changed ones.
Constitutions change on the order of years, via ballot measure.
State agency guidance is almost all insurance-regulator bulletins, and states are refreshed in rotation, about two a day, rather than all at once each week.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Statute exceptions">

The Colorado Revised Statutes are published once a year with no process for periodic updates, so a Colorado re-pull of all 46 titles changes the stored text only when a new edition appears.

Pennsylvania's Consolidated Statutes are complete.
Of its older unconsolidated statutes only 35 P.S. ch. 780 (54 sections) is held, and the rest is prioritized for backfill.

</Accordion>
