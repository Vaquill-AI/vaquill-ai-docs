## What's in the corpus

| Corpus | Jurisdictions | Sections | `corpusType` |
|---|---|---:|---|
| State statutes | 52 | 1,934,326 | `STATE` |
| State regulations | 52 | 1,467,437 | `REGULATION` |
| State court rules | 46 | 53,628 | `STATE_RULES` |
| State constitutions | 51 | 12,762 | `STATE_CONSTITUTION` |
| State agency guidance | 49 | 13,224 | `STATE_AGENCY_GUIDANCE` |
| Federal, 14 corpora | Federal | 1,399,898 | See [Federal](/api-guide/coverage/federal) |

Every section is indexed for hybrid semantic and keyword search, and mirrored in multiple formats
(HTML, PDF, XML, plain text, DOCX) where the source provides them.

<Note>
  Counts here are **distinct sections**, one per provision or document, matching what
  `GET /us/statutes/coverage` returns. Longer documents split into several passages for retrieval,
  so the index holds more entries than sections, over half of them Federal Register rules at 13 to
  15 passages each. That does not change what you get back: a citation lookup still resolves to
  one section with its full text.
</Note>

## Federal

**1,399,898 sections across 14 corpus types.** The US Code and the Statutes at Large, the CFR both
as a live compilation and as 31 published annual editions, Federal Register rules and a curated
slice of its notices, Executive Orders, agency guidance from 51 named sources, seven bodies of
administrative adjudication, the Federal Rules, the Constitution, the Sentencing Guidelines, tax
treaties and GovInfo statute compilations.

All **52 jurisdictions** carry statutes and regulations. 51 carry constitutions and 46 carry court

## State agency guidance

State-issued regulatory guidance that interprets state law, **13,224 documents across 49
jurisdictions**, almost all of it insurance-regulator bulletins. Queryable with
`corpusType=STATE_AGENCY_GUIDANCE` paired with `state`.

Unlike statutes and regulations this is **not** a comprehensive rollout: it lands state by state,
and some states may never be built.

## Refresh cadence

How often each corpus is re-pulled from its official source. Counts grow between updates, so call
`GET /us/statutes/coverage` for a live figure.

| Corpus | Cadence |
|---|---|
| Federal | Daily to weekly, and on amendment for the Constitution. See the [federal table](/api-guide/coverage/federal). |
| State statutes | 24 jurisdictions every 7 days, the rest monthly. See below. |
| State regulations | Monthly where the publisher supports one. Treat cadence as per-state, not uniform. |
| State court rules | Monthly, for the jurisdictions that carry them. |
| State constitutions | Monthly. Constitutions change on the order of years, via ballot measure. |

For the age of any single source, call [`GET /boards`](/api-guide/alerts). It is free and carries
`lastRetrievedAt`, `cadence` and `retrievalStatus` per source. `GET /us/statutes/coverage` reports
what each corpus is *current through* in the publisher's own words, which is a different question
and is not available for every state.

### Which statute cadence applies where

**Every 7 days, 24 jurisdictions.** Arkansas, California, Colorado, Florida, Georgia, Idaho,
Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Louisiana, Michigan, Mississippi, Missouri, Nevada, New Jersey, New York,
Oregon, Pennsylvania, Puerto Rico, South Dakota, Tennessee, Virginia, Wisconsin and Wyoming
publish their code in a form that can be pulled whole, an official bulk export, an API, or a
complete per-section fetch. Every run re-pulls the entire code and reconciles it against what is
stored rather than crawling for what looks new.

**Everywhere else**, statutes are re-pulled monthly or carry text as of their ingest date. That is
a fact you can read rather than assume: `GET /us/statutes/coverage` returns `lastRetrievedAt` for
every jurisdiction in its `retrieval` array, and anything we have deliberately stopped re-pulling
is declared `paused` in `freshness` with its reason.

<Note>
  **Colorado is checked weekly but re-pulled annually, and that is deliberate.** The Colorado
  Revised Statutes are published once a year with no process for periodic updates, so Colorado's
  weekly slot re-pulls all 46 titles only when a new edition appears.
</Note>

<Note>
  The `cadence` field on `/us/statutes/coverage` and `/boards` reports `monthly` for every state
  statute board, these 24 included. Read it as the declared staleness window a board is alerted
  against, not as a ceiling on how often the corpus is re-pulled.
</Note>

<Note>
  Pennsylvania's Consolidated Statutes are complete; its older unconsolidated (Purdon's) statutes
  are a separate later addition, prioritized for backfill.
</Note>

## Point-in-time and change history

Every corpus here can be asked what it said on a past date, and the answer states whether it is a
published edition or a reconstruction. See [Point-in-time law](/api-guide/point-in-time).

## Detecting a change

`GET /us/statutes/coverage` is self-describing, so new corpora appear there as they land. To find
sections that moved rather than corpora that appeared, use the `changedSince` filter on
`POST /us/statutes/search`. See
[Coverage monitoring](/api-guide/recipes/coverage-monitoring) for both patterns, and
[Law change alerts](/api-guide/alerts) to be pushed a change instead of polling for one.

Corpora that are planned but not yet served, and what is deliberately out of scope, are on the
[Roadmap](/api-guide/roadmap).
