## What's in the corpus

Counts as of **2026-11-07**.

| Corpus | Jurisdictions | Sections | `corpusType` | Refresh |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| State statutes | 56 | 2,642,636 | `STATE` | Monthly, quarterly for AR, GA, TN and VI |
| State regulations | 56 | 1,504,702 | `REGULATION` | Monthly |
| State court rules | 56 | 69,931 | `STATE_RULES` | Monthly |
| State constitutions | 53 | 13,057 | `STATE_CONSTITUTION` | Monthly |
| State registers (notices, not codified law) | 24 | 153,850 | `STATE_REGISTER` | Varies |
| State agency guidance | 49 | 13,402 | `STATE_AGENCY_GUIDANCE` | Monthly |
| State AG opinions | 45 | 109,438 | `STATE_AG_OPINION` | Monthly, new opinions only |
| Federal, 16 corpora | Federal | 1,534,416 | See [Federal](/api-guide/coverage/federal) and [Treaties](/api-guide/coverage/treaties) | Daily to weekly |
| [State session laws](/api-guide/session-laws) | 52 | See the endpoint | Own endpoints | Varies |

The 56 jurisdictions are the 50 states, DC, Puerto Rico, Guam, the Northern Mariana Islands, the US Virgin Islands and American Samoa.
All 56 carry statutes, regulations and court rules. 53 carry constitutions.
Session laws cover the 50 states, DC and Puerto Rico from 1975.

Every section is searchable by meaning and by keyword. Many are also available as HTML, PDF, XML, plain text or DOCX.

## Federal

Federal law has 16 corpus types, from the US Code, the CFR and the Federal Register to agency guidance, adjudications, the Federal Rules and treaties.
The [federal page](/api-guide/coverage/federal) lists each one. [Treaty coverage](/api-guide/coverage/treaties) covers the two treaty tokens.
Bill text of the 119th Congress is available as the opt-in `FEDERAL_BILL` token and is not part of any total.

Agency guidance has **53 named sources across 26 agencies** under `corpusType=AGENCY_GUIDANCE`. Narrow it with `source`.
See the [source registry](/api-guide/coverage/agency-guidance).

## State corpora

Per-jurisdiction counts for all 56 are in the [matrix](/api-guide/coverage/states).

### State administrative registers

`corpusType=STATE_REGISTER` holds notices a state printed in its official register: proposed, adopted and emergency rules.
It is not codified law and is served with `goodLawStatus: "unknown"`.
See [per-jurisdiction counts](/api-guide/coverage/states#state-administrative-registers).

### State agency guidance

State regulatory guidance that interprets state law, mostly insurance-regulator bulletins.
Query it with `corpusType=STATE_AGENCY_GUIDANCE` and `state`.
See [per-state sources](/api-guide/coverage/states#state-agency-guidance).

### State attorney general opinions

Formal written opinions of a state Attorney General on that state's statutes, constitution and regulations.
Query them with `corpusType=STATE_AG_OPINION` and `state`. 45 jurisdictions are live.

<Warning>
  **AG opinions are persuasive authority, not binding law.**

  See [Status and currency](/api-guide/concepts/status-and-currency).
</Warning>

Per-state counts are on [state coverage](/api-guide/coverage/states#state-attorney-general-opinions).

## Refresh cadence

The Refresh column above shows how often each corpus is updated from its official source.
For the age of any single source, call [`GET /boards`](/api-guide/alerts). It is free and returns `lastRetrievedAt` and `cadence` per source.
`GET /us/statutes/coverage` reports what each corpus is *current through*, in the publisher's own words.

### Which statute cadence applies where

Compare `lastRetrievedAt` with `cadence` on `GET /boards` to see how recently a source was updated.

- 46 jurisdictions report `cadence: "monthly"`.
- Arkansas, Georgia, Tennessee and the US Virgin Islands report `cadence: "quarterly"`.
- Delaware, Hawaii, Mississippi, New Mexico and Utah are covered but do not appear on `GET /boards`. Read their currency from `GET /us/statutes/coverage`.
- Colorado publishes once a year, so its text changes only when a new edition appears.
