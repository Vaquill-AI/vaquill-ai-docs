State and territory law for 50 states, DC, Puerto Rico, Guam, the Northern Mariana Islands, the US Virgin Islands and American Samoa.
One matrix holds every per-jurisdiction count.
Pair a `corpusType` filter with `state`:

```json
{ "query": "landlord retaliation", "corpusType": "STATE", "state": "ms" }
```

Pass a list to `state` to search several jurisdictions in one call.

## Per-jurisdiction matrix

[`GET /us/statutes/coverage`](/api-guide/coverage) returns the live figures.
A dash means the corpus is not held for that jurisdiction.
Statutes include past editions kept for point-in-time lookups. `statusBreakdown.superseded` in the coverage response gives the split.
Each column is a `corpusType` filter: Statutes is `STATE`, Regs is `REGULATION`, Const. is `STATE_CONSTITUTION`, Court rules is `STATE_RULES`, Registers is `STATE_REGISTER` and State agency guidance is `STATE_AGENCY_GUIDANCE`.

The Virgin Islands Code is an unofficial copy, so `sourceUrl` is null and `licenseNote` explains why.

## Sourcing notes

- **Vermont court rules.** Served from the consolidated text the Judiciary points to. Cite as `Vt. R. Civ. P. 26`. `sourceNote` carries the publisher's currency line.
- **Colorado court rules.** The 2023 volume is the base, with adopted orders applied forward. If an order could not be applied, the rule serves its 2023 text and `sourceNote` names the order. Eleven ICWA procedure rules carry `actStatus=suspended`.
- **Oklahoma court rules.** The Court rules count covers only the sets outside the statute book. Civil procedure, criminal procedure and evidence are under `corpusType=STATE`.
- **Uniform Commercial Code.** There is no separate UCC corpus. Each state's enacted version is in that state's statutes: search `corpusType: "STATE"` with `state`, and cite in the state's own numbering. Model section 2-207 is `Cal. Com. Code § 2207`, `6 Del. C. § 2-207` or `Tex. Bus. & Com. Code § 2.207`. The model text and Official Comments are not served.
- **UCC exceptions.** Louisiana never enacted Article 2. Puerto Rico did not adopt it either, and its Ley de Transacciones Comerciales is served under `state: "pr"` in Spanish.

## Court rules from county and district trial courts

Thirty-eight trial courts publish local rules.
They are served under `corpusType=STATE_RULES` with the county or district in the `actId`, so one county's Rule 6.5 is never another's.

## State administrative registers

`corpusType=STATE_REGISTER` holds notices a state printed in its official register: proposed, adopted and emergency rule text.
Each printed item is one document, cited by volume and page (`36 Pa. Bull. 510`).
The codified rule under `REGULATION` is the operative text.

<GoodLawUnknown />

`year` is the year of the issue the item was printed in.
Register items never outrank the rule they concern in search.
Search them with `{"corpusType": "STATE_REGISTER", "state": "pa"}`.

Held today: **153,850 items across 24 jurisdictions**.

A register citation also resolves when a codified rule's history note cites it.
See [Register citations](/api-guide/concepts/citation-formats#register-citations).

## State agency guidance

State regulatory guidance that interprets state law.
Query it with `corpusType=STATE_AGENCY_GUIDANCE` and `state`.

**13,402 documents across 49 jurisdictions.**

Almost all of it is insurance-regulator guidance (`source=state_insurance_bulletin`).
Arizona, Oregon, New Jersey and Vermont also publish financial-institutions guidance, served as `source=state_financial_bulletin`.
It covers banking, credit unions, escrow agents, consumer lenders and similar regulated businesses.
Its `goodLawStatus` is `unknown`.

[`GET /boards`](/api-guide/alerts) carries `lastRetrievedAt` per state.

NAIC model law text is not included.

## State attorney general opinions

Formal written opinions in which a state's chief legal officer construes that state's laws for an officer, legislator or local government.
Query it with `corpusType=STATE_AG_OPINION` and `state`.

**109,438 opinions across 45 jurisdictions.**

<Warning>
  Every opinion is persuasive authority and is served with `goodLawStatus: "unknown"`.
  Montana and Ohio opinions carry no issue date, so they cannot answer an `asOf` query.
  Find them by citation, volume or search.
</Warning>

Where a jurisdiction has more than one series, the `series` field carries the publisher's label, for example Washington `AGO` and `AGLO`, California `OP`, `CV`, `CR` and `SO`, and Georgia Official and Unofficial.

Florida opinions from 1913 to 1991 are citation and metadata only, marked `textAvailability: "metadata_only"`. Filter on that field to include or exclude them.
