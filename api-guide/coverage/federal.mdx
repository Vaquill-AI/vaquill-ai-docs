## Tokens and scope

Pass the `corpusType` token to filter a search to one corpus.
All tokens are listed in [Corpus types](/api-guide/concepts/corpus-types).
Open the entry below for what a corpus holds.

| Corpus | `corpusType` | Scope entry |
|---|---|---|
| United States Code | `USC` | United States Code |
| Statutes at Large | `SESSION_LAW` | Statutes at Large |
| CFR, including FAR and DFARS | `CFR` | None beyond the table |
| CFR annual editions | `CFR_ANNUAL` | CFR annual editions |
| Federal Register agency rules | `FEDERAL_REGISTER` | None beyond the table |
| Federal Register notices | `FEDERAL_REGISTER_NOTICE` | Federal Register notices |
| Executive Orders | `EXECUTIVE_ACTION` | None beyond the table |
| Agency guidance | `AGENCY_GUIDANCE` | [Federal agency guidance sources](/api-guide/coverage/agency-guidance) |
| Federal Rules of Procedure | `FEDERAL_RULES` | Federal Rules of Procedure |
| Federal local court rules | `FEDERAL_LOCAL_RULES` | Federal local court rules |
| US Constitution | `CONSTITUTION` | None beyond the table |
| US Sentencing Guidelines | `SENTENCING_GUIDELINES` | US Sentencing Guidelines |
| US Tax Treaties | `US_TAX_TREATY` | Treaties |
| The Senate treaty record | `US_TREATY` | Treaties |
| Administrative Adjudications | `AGENCY_ADJUDICATION` | Administrative adjudications |
| Statute Compilations | `STATUTE_COMPILATION` | Statute Compilations |

Bills and resolutions of the 119th Congress are available under the opt-in token `corpusType=FEDERAL_BILL`, as the latest printed version of each measure.
It is proposed text only: `actStatus` is `proposed` and `goodLawStatus` is `not_good_law` on every row. It is never part of an unscoped search or a total.
Sponsors, actions and committees are not held, so the stage on a row describes the text version, not where the bill stands.
Enacted bills are served as public laws under `corpusType=SESSION_LAW` and, once codified, under `corpusType=USC`.
`POST /us/statutes/resolve` resolves a bill by citation with the Congress named, such as `H.R. 1, 119th Cong.`.
A bill cited without its Congress, such as `H.R. 1`, is refused with a hint to add it.

<Accordion title="United States Code: current release point">
  Served from the Office of the Law Revision Counsel's release points, checked weekly.
  Every section has a `currentThrough` date, the date of the current release point.

  For federal law enacted after that date, use `corpusType=SESSION_LAW` or `corpusType=FEDERAL_REGISTER` for agency rulemaking.
  The last GPO annual edition is kept as `USC_ANNUAL` so `asOf` can answer for earlier dates. It is not a token to search.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Statutes at Large: laws as enacted">
  Federal public and private laws as enacted, 1789-2026.
  A US Code section's `publicLawCites` resolve here to the enacting text.

  Sections resolve by `actId`, for example `SAL_PL116-136_DVA_TII_S1109` (CARES Act section 1109).
  Treaty text printed in the Statutes at Large is not served here.

  These are historical records, not current law. They carry `actStatus: "enacted"` and `goodLawStatus: "unknown"`.
  For what the law says today, use `corpusType=USC`.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="CFR annual editions: published editions">
  GPO's annual edition of the CFR, from 1996 to 2026, all 49 titles.
  It is the same regulation as the `CFR` corpus, seen at published dates. It adds no new sections to the federal total.

  - `editionsObserved` lists exact years, never a range. A date in a gap returns `asOf.isBounded: false` instead of the nearest year.
  - A citation year is the volume's own `<REVISED>` year, not the folder GPO filed it under.

  `CFR_ANNUAL` serves an edition as published. `asOf` on `GET /us/statutes/section/{actId}/body` reconstructs a section from change history.
  See [Point-in-time law](/api-guide/point-in-time).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Federal Register notices: notices on a rulemaking">
  **Held:** notices that act on a rulemaking. These withdraw a proposed rule, extend or reopen a comment period, publish significant guidance, or announce negotiated rulemaking.

  **Not held:** information collections, meeting notices, permits, antidumping proceedings and Privacy Act notices.

  Filter by docket (`frDocketIds`), not by CFR citation. A notice amends nothing, so `amendmentYears` is empty and `goodLawStatus` is `unknown`.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Administrative adjudications: seven bodies">
  Federal matters decided by an agency through a formal process.

  | Body | Sections | `source` |
  |---|---:|---|
  | FTC Part 3 administrative filings, 1995 to date | 18,023 | `ftc_administrative_decision` |
  | SEC opinions and adjudicatory orders, 1996 to date | 5,790 | `sec_commission_opinion` |
  | Merit Systems Protection Board | 5,461 | `mspb_precedential`, `mspb_nonprecedential` |
  | BIA and Attorney General precedent decisions | 3,292 | `bia_precedent`, `ag_precedent`, `immigration_admin_precedent` |
  | NLRB Board decisions, 1935 to date | 66,597 | `nlrb_board_decision` |
  | DOJ Office of Legal Counsel | 1,371 | `olc_opinion` |
  | CFPB enforcement actions | 386 | `cfpb_enforcement_action` |

  Citation forms: `FTC Docket 9436`, `Exchange Act Release No. 34-106254`, `YYYY MSPB N`, `I&N Dec.`, `NNN NLRB No. N`, `NN Op. O.L.C. NNN`, Bureau File No. `2024-CFPB-0002`.

  `goodLawStatus` is `unknown` on every body except the CFPB slice. A decision is served as issued, with no overruling signal.

  Not held: OLC opinions from 2021 on, SEC ALJ initial decisions, BIA decisions before I&N Dec. volume 8, and FTC court records. FTC rules are under `corpusType=CFR`.
  The FTC slice is Part 3 administrative filings only.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Treaties: US_TAX_TREATY and US_TREATY">
  `corpusType=US_TAX_TREATY` holds IRS income tax treaties. `corpusType=US_TREATY` holds treaty text and the Senate treaty record.
  See [treaty coverage](/api-guide/coverage/treaties).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Federal Rules of Procedure: committee notes, past texts and pending amendments">
  The national Rules of Civil, Criminal, Appellate and Bankruptcy Procedure, the Rules of Evidence and the Supreme Court Rules, under `corpusType=FEDERAL_RULES`.

  Three opt-in layers sit beside the current text: committee notes and the 1937 print, past texts under `FEDERAL_RULES_ANNUAL`, and pending amendments under `FEDERAL_RULES_PROPOSED`.

  <Expandable title="Committee notes (committeeNotes)">
  `GET /us/statutes/section/{actId}/body` returns `committeeNotes`, the Committee Note split into one entry per amendment.
  Each entry has `year`, `kind`, `heading`, `text`, `effectiveDate` and `subdivisionsDiscussed`.
  The publisher's own notes (References in Text, Amendment by Public Law, Effective Date) come as `editorial` entries.
  `committeeNotes` is null when the split could not reproduce `notes` exactly.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="The 1937 note as printed (committeeNote1937Print)">
  For a Civil Rule that existed in 1938, `committeeNote1937Print` gives the original Advisory Committee note as Congress printed it (H.R. Doc. No. 588, 75th Cong., 3d Sess. (1938)).
  Its `verdict` compares the print with the 1937 note in `notes`.
  Values are `agrees`, `differs`, `none_printed`, or `print_only` (the print has a note and `notes` has none).

  `printText` is OCR of the print.
  `printTextStatus` is `verified` only when the print agrees, and `unverified_ocr` otherwise. Quote the scanned page (`sourceUrl`, `pageStart`, `pageEnd`, `sourceSha256`), not the string.
  `ocrMethod` and `ocrConfidenceMean` say how it was read.
  `format=operative` drops both fields, as it drops `notes`.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Past texts (FEDERAL_RULES_ANNUAL)">
  `corpusType=FEDERAL_RULES_ANNUAL` holds superseded texts from GPO's annual editions, under the `actId` form `{current actId}_E{year}`.
  It is opt-in and not watchable. `asOf` on a rule's `/body` answers from these texts.
  The record begins 1998-01-26. An earlier date returns no text (`available: false`, `source: "unavailable"`, not charged) with a note naming the earliest edition.
  See [Point-in-time law](/api-guide/point-in-time).
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Pending amendments (FEDERAL_RULES_PROPOSED)">
  `corpusType=FEDERAL_RULES_PROPOSED` holds amendment documents, under the `actId` form `{current actId}_P{cycle year}`.
  A proposal with no current rule is held under the id it would take. One with no cycle year yet ends `_PNOCYCLE`, for example `FRULES_FRE_R707_PNOCYCLE`.
  Each document is `proposed`, `superseded` (the amendment took effect) or `withdrawn`. Read `actStatus` before treating a row as pending.
  It is opt-in and refreshed weekly. A proposal asked with `asOf` is refused.
  A statute result for the rule in force lists proposals in `pendingAmendments`:

  | Field | Meaning |
  |---|---|
  | `id` | The `actId` of the proposed amendment. Fetch it with `/section/{id}` or `/body` |
  | `status` | `published_for_comment`, `approved_by_standing_committee`, `approved_by_judicial_conference`, `transmitted_to_supreme_court`, `adopted_by_supreme_court` or `transmitted_to_congress` |
  | `cycle` | The rules cycle, the year the amendment is meant to take effect |
  | `effectiveDate` | The date it takes effect if adopted unchanged |
  | `commentPeriodEnd` | Last day of the public comment period, when one applies |
  | `sourceUrl` | The official page it was taken from, when we hold a link we may publish |
  | `textComplete` | `true` when the proposed text is the whole amended rule, `false` when only the changed paragraphs |
  | `commentDocketIds` | Dockets that hold the public comments, each readable on `GET /us/rulemaking/dockets/{docketId}` |

  `pendingAmendments` appears only on a rule with a pending proposal.
  To be told when a proposal moves a stage, watch the rule: see [alerts](/api-guide/alerts#pending-federal-rules-amendments).
  </Expandable>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Federal local court rules: each court's own rules">
  The rules a federal court publishes for itself, under `corpusType=FEDERAL_LOCAL_RULES`. 52 courts are covered.

  | Group | Courts |
  |---|---:|
  | Courts of appeals | 13 |
  | Habeas rules (Sections 2254 and 2255) | 1 |
  | National bodies: Armed Forces, Federal Claims, Tax Court, International Trade, Veterans Claims, and the Manual for Courts-Martial | 6 |
  | District courts | 32 |

  Not held: the other district courts, local bankruptcy rules, and the internal operating procedures of the Fifth, Seventh, Eighth and Eleventh Circuits.

  Reserved and repealed rule numbers say so in `actStatus`.
  A bare number such as `Cir. R. 36-3` names no court. A district with separate civil and criminal sets needs the set in the citation.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Statute Compilations: an act as amended">
  An act's own text as amended through a stated later public law, from GovInfo's official compilations.
  They follow the act, not the code. A citation like "Section 210 of the Public Utility Regulatory Policies Act" resolves here even though that section has no `USC` address.

  Statutes at Large holds the same laws in their original form.
  Each section states its currency in `currentThrough`, from `Public Law 96-308` (1980) to `Public Law 119-75` (2026). Some state none.
  Cross-references into `USC` are in `crossReferencesUsc`.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="US Sentencing Guidelines: former versions">
  The 302 current guideline sections, plus Appendix A (the statutory index) and Appendix B.
  Appendix B, Part III holds former guidelines and commentary as separate documents.
</Accordion>
