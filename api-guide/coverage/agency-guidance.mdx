Sub-regulatory federal guidance: what an agency says its own rules mean, short of a regulation.
**53 named sources across 26 agencies.**
All sit behind the single `corpusType=AGENCY_GUIDANCE` token.
Narrow to one with `source`.

```json
{ "query": "tip credit", "corpusType": "AGENCY_GUIDANCE", "source": "whd_foh" }
```

State insurance and financial bulletins are a different corpus: see the [state coverage matrix](/api-guide/coverage/states).

<Note>
  The counts below cover the 53 named sources (87,082 documents).
  The token's own total is in the [federal coverage table](/api-guide/coverage/federal).
  `GET /us/statutes/coverage` returns the live total and the list of sources, and wins where this page differs.
  An invalid `source` returns the full list with each code's name.
</Note>

## goodLawStatus by source

Where a publisher states no retirement record for its guidance, `goodLawStatus` is `unknown`.
See [status and currency](/api-guide/concepts/status-and-currency).

| `source` | `goodLawStatus` |
|---|---|
| `irs_irm`, `cms_iom`, `whd_foh`, `doj_business_review` | `unknown` |
| `ftc_advisory_opinion`, `ftc_policy_statement`, `ftc_hsr_interpretation`, `ftc_advocacy_filing` | `unknown` |
| `irs_written_determination`, `whd_opinion_letter`, `eeoc_guidance`, `ofac_faq`, `fincen_boi_rule_qa` | Read from source |

## Source notes

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="irs_written_determination: not citable as precedent">
  Private Letter Rulings, Chief Counsel Advice, Technical Advice Memoranda and similar determinations under 26 U.S.C. § 6110.
  Each is directed to one taxpayer and "may not be used or cited as precedent", so every document carries `actStatus: "non_precedential"`.

  Some are scans read by OCR, so a passage may read `section 501(c)(3)` where the page prints `§ 501(c)(3)`. Search for either.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="irs_irm: internal IRS procedure">
  The IRS's instruction manual for its employees.
  Every passage is labeled "IRS internal procedure".
  Do not read an IRM response timeframe as a statutory deadline.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="cms_iom: Medicare operating manuals">
  What a provider may bill, what Medicare covers, and what a contractor reads before an audit.
  Manual provisions are not binding rules, and every passage says so in `currencyNote`.
  CMS replaces chapters in place, so `asOf` is not bounded for this source.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="whd_foh: enforcement handbook">
  How the Wage and Hour Division tells its investigators to read the FLSA, FMLA and related laws.
  WHD says these pages may not reflect current legislation, regulations or court decisions.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="whd_opinion_letter: withdrawal status is published">
  WHD's written answers to a named requester's question, mostly FLSA and FMLA.
  `actStatus` is read from DOL's own footnote table and disclaimer lines: `in_force`, `withdrawn`, `superseded` or `vacated`.
  A letter re-issued under a new number is its own document, and the earlier one stays retrievable as withdrawn.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="eeoc_guidance: status comes from EEOC's banner">
  The Commission's interpretation of the statutes it enforces, including Compliance Manual sections.
  `goodLawStatus` is read from the retirement banner EEOC prints on a document.
  Guidance EEOC stopped relying on without a banner reads `in_force`.
  Federal-sector appellate decisions are under `corpusType=AGENCY_ADJUDICATION`.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="ftc_advisory_opinion: binds only the requester">
  Commission and staff answers to proposed conduct under 16 C.F.R. §§ 1.1-1.4.
  An advisory opinion decides no case, so it sits here and not under `AGENCY_ADJUDICATION`.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="ftc_hsr_interpretation: formal and informal">
  Hart-Scott-Rodino premerger interpretations, under one `source`.
  Filter the two kinds apart with `documentSubtype`: formal (the Commission's own construction) and informal (staff answers, not binding on the Commission).
  Join them on `construedRule`.
  Supersession is served as `supersedes` and `supersededBy`, and no document is marked dead because of it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="ftc_advocacy_filing: binds nobody">
  Letters and comments from the Commission and its staff to legislatures, courts and agencies about the competition effects of a proposed measure.
  They decide no case.
  Scope them out with `forum` (`ftc_advocacy`).
  The Matter Number is a program-level number and is served as `caseNumber`, not as a citation.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="copyright_compendium: point-in-time by edition">
  The manual the Copyright Office applies to registrations, in three editions (2014, 2017, 2021).
  `asOf` resolves to the edition in force on that date.
  That is the manual on that date, not the law.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="doj_business_review: no withdrawal signal">
  The Antitrust Division's statements of enforcement intention on proposed conduct under 28 C.F.R. § 50.6.
  Each is served as the response letter with the requester's letter appended.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="ofac_faq: prior versions are separate documents">
  OFAC's interpretation of the sanctions regulations at 31 C.F.R. chapter V.
  Prior versions are separate, citable documents with `actStatus: "superseded"`, so `asOf` returns the text OFAC published on that date.
  History starts 2023-03-22.
  Sanctions lists and General Licenses are not included.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="administrative_guidance: reference figures">
  Annually adjusted dollar thresholds that do not live in USC or CFR text, such as the SALT cap, the Child Tax Credit, 401(k) limits and the Subchapter V debt limit.
  Each is primary-sourced but assembled by Vaquill, not published as one agency document.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="fincen_boi_*: the August 2026 final rule">
  On August 11, 2026 FinCEN narrowed BOI reporting.
  Entities created in the United States and their beneficial owners are exempt.
  Only certain companies formed under foreign law and registered in a US State or tribal jurisdiction must report.

  `fincen_boi_faq` and `fincen_boi_compliance_guide` predate the rule.
  They are served as published, with FinCEN's notice in `currencyNote`.
  Disregard any statement in them that US companies or US persons must report BOI.

  For the current position use `fincen_boi_rule_qa`.
  Its 10 interim-rule Q&As are `superseded` and excluded from good-law retrieval.
</Accordion>
