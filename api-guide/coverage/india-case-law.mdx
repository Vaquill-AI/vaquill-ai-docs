That is 34,941 Supreme Court judgments and 12,793,684 judgments from 25 High Courts.

```bash
curl https://api.vaquill.ai/api/v1/in/courts \

```

Every count on this page is a dated measurement, as of 2026-11-10.
The live `GET /in/courts` route is authoritative whenever the two disagree, because this page is written by hand and the route reads the registry.

## What a judgment is here

A **judgment** is one decided case, with one stable `caseId`.
Supreme Court ids look like `2020_INSC_548`.
High Court ids carry a court prefix and a registry number, such as `MPHC020138372016`.

Search returns judgments, not passages.
The same judgment is never returned twice, even where it is stored in more than one place.

Do not read a judgment count as a passage count.
Each judgment is stored as many passages, and the full text is stitched back from them when you ask for `/body`.

## Routes

Nine operations under `https://api.vaquill.ai/api/v1/in`.
The Credits column is a snapshot and the live pricing matrix is authoritative.

| Route, under `/api/v1/in` | Credits | What it answers |
|---|---:|---|
| `GET /courts` | **0** | Which courts are held, with judgment counts and date range. |
| `GET /cases/list` | 1 | Browse judgments by court and date, newest first. |
| `GET /cases/search` | 2 | Search judgments by meaning and keyword, one result per judgment. |
| `GET /cases/{caseId}/detail` | 1 | One judgment's case-level record. |
| `GET /cases/{caseId}/body` | 3 | The full judgment text, stitched from its passages. |
| `GET /cases/{caseId}/file` | 1 | The archived file as bytes. |
| `GET /cases/resolve` | 2 | One citation or case number to one judgment, exact only. |
| `POST /cases/resolve` | 2 per distinct input | Up to 100 citations or case numbers, with a per-item outcome. |
| `GET /cases/{caseId}/similar` | 2 | The judgments semantically closest to one you hold. |

Full request and response shapes are in the India API Reference tab, generated from the [India OpenAPI document](https://api.vaquill.ai/in/openapi.json).

## Known limits

Read this section before quoting a number or a date to a client.

**Decision dates are checked, and some are withheld.**
A stored date that is later than today, earlier than 1800, or, for a High Court judgment, earlier than the filing year carried in its own case id, is not served as a date.
Those judgments answer `decisionDate: null` and `dateQuality: "implausible"`.
A judgment with no stored date, or one that does not parse, answers `dateQuality: "missing"`.
We withhold rather than guess, so a date filter excludes these judgments, and read `dateQuality` before treating a date as exact.

**`reportedYear` is not the decision year.**
For Supreme Court judgments it is the year of the reporter volume.
It differs from the decision year on about 15% of Supreme Court judgments, which is why it is served under its own name.

**Reporter citations exist only for the Supreme Court.**
Supreme Court judgments carry the Supreme Court Reports form, such as `[1982] 2 S.C.R. 186`.
High Court judgments carry no reporter citation, and `reporterCitations` is empty for them.
We do not construct one.

**A body can be incomplete, and says so.**
`/body` reports `complete`, `chunkCount`, `expectedChunks` and `missingChunkIndexes`.
When `complete` is `false`, the missing passages are named and the call is refunded.

**Results can be partial.**
Judgments are held in two stores.
If one fails during a search or a similar-judgments call, the answer is built from the other and carries `meta.partial: true`.
If both fail the answer is a 503, refunded, never an empty page.

**Resolve refuses rather than guesses.**
Matching is exact.
A citation that matches nothing is `not_found`, and one that matches several judgments is `ambiguous` with every match listed.
Case numbers are stored as bare `number/year` and are not unique across case types, so `ambiguous` is common for them.
SCC, AIR, SCC OnLine, MANU and LiveLaw citations are refused with a 422 `unsupported_citation_form`, and `2023 INSC 123` style neutral citations are not accepted.

**`/file` answers bytes, not JSON.**
When no file was archived for a judgment it answers a 404 `case_file_unavailable`, and the credit is refunded.
Check `hasFile` on the detail record first.

**Some judgments cannot be reached by a court filter.**
A small number of raw court labels, such as "High Court of Hyderabad", cannot be assigned to one court and are not counted under any `courtId`.
Those judgments are reachable by search, but not by a `court` filter.

**Search has a depth, and no total.**
Search is honest to 200 results per query, and neither `/search` nor `/list` returns a `total`.
Both page with a cursor.

**What this surface does not hold.**
There is no citation graph, no cited-by list, no good-law or treatment status, no headnotes, no judge filter and no ask or summarise route.
Those fields are absent rather than filled with a default.

**Counts move.**
The corpus is extended over time, so a figure on this page is a measurement rather than a constant.
Anything you display to a user should come from `/courts`.
