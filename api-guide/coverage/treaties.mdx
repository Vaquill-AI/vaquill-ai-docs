Treaty material sits behind two `corpusType` tokens.
`US_TAX_TREATY` holds the IRS income tax treaties.
`US_TREATY` holds treaty and international agreement text, and the Senate treaty record.
A search for one never returns the other.

## What the two tokens hold

Narrow either token to one body of material with `source`.
Treaty text is served one document per article, so one instrument is many documents.

| Token | `source` | Documents | What it is |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `US_TAX_TREATY` | `us_tax_treaty` | 102 | Income tax conventions, protocols, exchanges of notes, and related documents |
| `US_TAX_TREATY` | `us_tax_treaty_technical_explanation` | 71 | Treasury Technical Explanations |
| `US_TREATY` | `us_treaty` | 133,865 | Treaty and international agreement text, one document per article, from seven series |
| `US_TREATY` | `senate_treaty` | 782 | One record for each treaty the Senate has considered since 1949: title, parties, subjects and procedural history |
| `US_TREATY` | `senate_treaty_resolution` | 407 | The resolution of advice and consent, with reservations, understandings and declarations |
| `US_TREATY` | `senate_treaty_document` | 303 | The President's transmittal text, for treaties sent to the Senate from 1995 on |

Counts as of 2026-11-08.

### Treaty text by series

| Series | Instruments | Cite as |
|---|---:|---|
| Statutes at Large, volumes 1 to 64 (1776 to 1949) | 2,778 | `49 Stat. 3000` |
| U.S.T., volumes 1 to 35 (1950 to 1984) | 8,936 | `20 U.S.T. 361` |
| T.I.A.S., from 1984 on | 3,005 | `T.I.A.S. No. 24-815.1` |
| Bevans, instruments not also printed in the Statutes at Large | 610 | `1 Bevans 278` |
| Social Security totalization agreements | 27 | `Social security totalization agreement, Norway, agreement, art. 2`, or its T.I.A.S. number |
| FATCA intergovernmental agreements | 192 | Search by country |
| U.S. free trade agreements, with their tariff schedules | 360 | Search by agreement |

A Bevans citation to an instrument also printed in the Statutes at Large answers with the Statutes at Large printing.

## Searching and filtering

Both tokens use the same search route, `POST /us/statutes/search`, and the same `corpusType` and `source` filters described in [Corpus types](/api-guide/concepts/corpus-types).
`corpusType` also accepts a list, so one call can search both tokens.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Treaty text">
    Find an article of a treaty by what it says:

    ```json
    { "query": "service of judicial documents by postal channels", "corpusType": "US_TREATY", "source": "us_treaty" }
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Income tax treaty">
    Find a withholding rule in the text of an IRS income tax treaty or protocol, leaving out the Technical Explanations:

    ```json
    { "query": "dividends withholding rate limitation on benefits", "corpusType": "US_TAX_TREATY", "source": "us_tax_treaty" }
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Technical Explanation">
    Read the Treasury's explanation of a treaty instead of the treaty itself:

    ```json
    { "query": "Technical Explanation permanent establishment", "corpusType": "US_TAX_TREATY", "source": "us_tax_treaty_technical_explanation" }
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Senate resolution">
    Find the reservations and understandings the Senate attached to its consent:

    ```json
    { "query": "understanding declaration extradition", "corpusType": "US_TREATY", "source": "senate_treaty_resolution" }
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Both tokens">
    Search both tokens in one call:

    ```json
    { "query": "double taxation relief", "corpusType": ["US_TAX_TREATY", "US_TREATY"] }
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Citations

A treaty citation is always recognized as a treaty.
It is never read as a U.S. Code section or a Public Law.
The full list of accepted forms is in [Treaty citations](/api-guide/concepts/citation-formats#treaty-citations).

| Citation | Result |
|---|---|
| U.S.T., such as `20 U.S.T. 361` | Resolves to the instrument that begins at that page, or the one instrument whose pages span it: its opening section, with every article listed in `container.children` |
| Statutes at Large pages in volumes 1 to 64, such as `49 Stat. 3000`, and the lettered treaty pages of volumes 61 and 64, such as `64 Stat. B-81` | Resolves to the treaty or the session law printed at that page. A page where an act begins and the act before it may end is refused when we cannot name both |
| Bevans, such as `1 Bevans 631` | Resolves to the instrument, from its Statutes at Large printing where both exist |
| T.I.A.S. from 1984 on, such as `T.I.A.S. No. 95-1121` or `T.I.A.S. No. 24-815.1` | Resolves to the instrument with that number. An older number, such as `T.I.A.S. No. 6638`, resolves through its U.S.T. printing |
| A totalization agreement, such as `Social security totalization agreement, Norway, agreement, art. 2` | Resolves to that article. The agreement cite alone resolves to the instrument. A supplementary agreement or protocol is cited by its own kind, such as `Social security totalization agreement, Austria, supplementary agreement` |
| An IRS tax treaty document by its own citation, such as `U.S.-Canada Income Tax Treaty (1980)` or `U.S.-Spain Tax Treaty Technical Explanation (2014)` | Resolves to that document. A convention cited without a year, such as `U.S.-U.K. Income Tax Convention`, resolves only when we hold one convention for that country |
| Any of the above with an article, such as `Hague Service Convention art. 10, Nov. 15, 1965, 20 U.S.T. 361` | Resolves to that article. An article the instrument does not print is refused, never answered with another |
| A U.S.T. or Statutes at Large cite with a pin page, such as `20 U.S.T. 361, 365` | `pagePinpoint` reports the page. When exactly one article is printed on that page, the answer is that article. When several articles begin on that page, the answer is the instrument and `pagePinpoint.sections` lists them. A page outside the instrument is reported, never guessed |
| Senate Treaty Document, such as `S. Treaty Doc. No. 98-9` | Resolves to the Senate record for that treaty: the Treaty Document text when the treaty went to the Senate from 1995 on, otherwise the ratification record |
| T.S. and E.A.S. numbers, such as `T.S. No. 993` or `E.A.S. No. 12` | Resolves to the instrument through its Statutes at Large printing: `T.S. No. 993` is the Charter of the United Nations at `59 Stat. 1031`. A number whose Statutes at Large printing we do not hold is `not_held` |
| U.N.T.S. and L.N.T.S., such as `658 U.N.T.S. 163`, and Senate Executive Reports, such as `S. Exec. Rep. No. 102-23` | `resolved: false`, `missReason: "not_held"`, with a `missReasonHint` |

The `missReasonHint` lists the treaty material we hold.
A citation that two instruments share is refused, never answered with one of them.

## Limits

- Text printed only in U.N.T.S. or L.N.T.S. is not held, and neither are Senate Executive Reports.
- A T.S., E.A.S. or pre-1984 T.I.A.S. number resolves only where we hold the instrument's Statutes at Large or U.S.T. printing.
- Some Senate records are metadata with no instrument text. `source` tells you which.
- `goodLawStatus` is `unknown` on every treaty document.
- `US_TAX_TREATY` covers income tax only.

For the other federal tokens, see [Federal coverage](/api-guide/coverage/federal).
