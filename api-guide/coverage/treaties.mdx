Treaty material sits behind two `corpusType` tokens.
`US_TAX_TREATY` holds the IRS income tax treaties.
`US_TREATY` holds treaty and international agreement text, and the Senate treaty record.
A search for one never returns the other.

## What the two tokens hold

Narrow either token to one body of material with `source`.
A document is one citable item, and a retrieval passage is one searchable chunk of it.
Treaty text is served one document per article, so one instrument is many documents.

| Token | `source` | Documents | What it is | Origin |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| `US_TAX_TREATY` | `us_tax_treaty` | 102 | Bilateral income tax conventions, protocols, exchanges of notes, the memorandum of understanding and the negotiation statement | The IRS tax treaty pages |
| `US_TAX_TREATY` | `us_tax_treaty_technical_explanation` | 71 | Treasury Technical Explanations | The IRS tax treaty pages |
| `US_TREATY` | `us_treaty` | 100,435 | Treaty and international agreement text, one document per article, from five printed series (see the next table) | GPO, the Library of Congress, the Treasury and USTR |
| `US_TREATY` | `senate_treaty` | 782 | One record for each treaty the Senate has considered since 1949, tax and non-tax: title, parties, subject index and dated procedural history | The Senate treaty record |
| `US_TREATY` | `senate_treaty_resolution` | 407 | The resolution of advice and consent, where the reservations, understandings and declarations live | The Senate treaty record |
| `US_TREATY` | `senate_treaty_document` | 303 | The President's transmittal text, for treaties sent to the Senate from 1995 (the 104th Congress) on | GPO, the Government Publishing Office |

Counts for `us_treaty` measured 2026-11-07; the others 2026-11-06.

### Treaty text by series

| Series | Instruments | Documents (articles) | Retrieval passages | Cite as |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Statutes at Large, volumes 1 to 64 (1776 to 1949) | 2,779 | 31,839 | 44,251 | `49 Stat. 3000` |
| U.S.T., volumes 1 to 35 (1950 to 1984) | 8,936 | 57,424 | 115,644 | `20 U.S.T. 361` |
| Bevans, instruments not also printed in the Statutes at Large | 610 | 2,073 | 4,182 | `5 Bevans 123` |
| FATCA intergovernmental agreements | 192 | 1,850 | 10,808 | Search by country |
| U.S. free trade agreements, with their tariff schedules | 361 | 7,249 | 27,646 | Search by agreement |

A Bevans citation to an instrument that the Statutes at Large also printed answers with the Statutes at Large printing.

The 102 documents of `us_tax_treaty` are 71 bilateral income tax conventions for 68 countries, 23 protocols, 6 exchanges of notes, 1 memorandum of understanding and 1 negotiation statement.
A protocol is served as its own document rather than folded into the convention it amends.
That keeps each protocol searchable on its own terms, such as a change to a withholding rate, a limitation-on-benefits article or an entry-into-force date.
The 303 documents of `senate_treaty_document` are 285 numbered Treaty Documents and 18 Serial Set packages.

## Searching and filtering

Both tokens use the same search route, `POST /us/statutes/search`, and the same `corpusType` and `source` filters described in [Corpus types](/api-guide/concepts/corpus-types).
`corpusType` also accepts a list, so one call can search both tokens.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Treaty text">
    Find an article of a treaty by what it says:

    ```json
    { "query": "service of judicial documents by postal channels", "corpusType": "US_TREATY", "source": "us_treaty" }
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Income tax treaty">
    Find a withholding rule in an IRS income tax treaty:

    ```json
    { "query": "dividends withholding rate limitation on benefits", "corpusType": "US_TAX_TREATY" }
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Technical Explanation">
    Read the Treasury's explanation of a treaty instead of the treaty itself:

    ```json
    { "query": "Technical Explanation permanent establishment", "corpusType": "US_TAX_TREATY", "source": "us_tax_treaty_technical_explanation" }
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Senate resolution">
    Find the reservations and understandings the Senate attached to its consent:

    ```json
    { "query": "understanding declaration extradition", "corpusType": "US_TREATY", "source": "senate_treaty_resolution" }
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Both tokens">
    Search both tokens in one call:

    ```json
    { "query": "double taxation relief", "corpusType": ["US_TAX_TREATY", "US_TREATY"] }
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Citations

A treaty citation is always recognized as a treaty.
It is never read as a U.S. Code section or a Public Law.
The full list of accepted forms is in [Treaty citations](/api-guide/concepts/citation-formats#treaty-citations).

| Citation | Result |
|---|---|
| U.S.T., such as `20 U.S.T. 361` | Resolves to the instrument that begins at that page: its opening section, with every article listed in `container.children` |
| Statutes at Large pages in volumes 1 to 64, such as `49 Stat. 3000` | Resolves to the treaty or the session law printed at that page |
| Bevans, such as `1 Bevans 631` | Resolves to the instrument, from its Statutes at Large printing where both exist |
| Any of the above with an article, such as `Hague Service Convention art. 10, Nov. 15, 1965, 20 U.S.T. 361` | Resolves to that article. An article the instrument does not print is refused, never answered with another |
| Senate Treaty Document, such as `S. Treaty Doc. No. 98-9` | Resolves to the Senate record for that treaty: the Treaty Document text when the treaty went to the Senate from 1995 on, otherwise the ratification record |
| T.I.A.S., T.S., E.A.S., U.N.T.S., L.N.T.S., such as `T.I.A.S. No. 6638`, and Senate Executive Reports, such as `S. Exec. Rep. No. 102-23` | `resolved: false`, `missReason: "not_held"`, with a `missReasonHint` |

The `missReasonHint` states that the form was recognized and lists the treaty material we hold.
A miss on a series we hold says so, and asks you to check the volume and page.

## Limits

- Text printed only in T.I.A.S. (from 1985), and Senate Executive Reports, are not held.
- Treaty text is the printed text, read from page scans for U.S.T. and Bevans. A bilingual printing can carry the other authentic language next to the English.
- About 60% of the Senate record is metadata with no instrument text. `source` tells you which records those are.
- `goodLawStatus` is `unknown` on every treaty document. No source we hold states termination.
- `US_TAX_TREATY` covers income tax only. There are no estate or gift tax treaties.
