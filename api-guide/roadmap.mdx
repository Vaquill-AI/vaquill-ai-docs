This page lists what is not built yet. It shows direction only, and no item has a committed date.

To see what is queryable now, call `GET /us/statutes/coverage`. It lists every jurisdiction and corpus, is free, and needs only your API key.
Poll it to detect new corpora. New corpora use the same `corpusType` filter, so there is nothing new to integrate.

## Not built yet

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Planned | Intended, with no date. |
| On demand | Added if customers ask for it. |
| Evaluating | Under consideration. |
| Not planned | No current plan. |

| Area | Source | What it adds | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Existing coverage | State agency guidance (`STATE_AGENCY_GUIDANCE`): Pennsylvania, Colorado, Louisiana | Insurance guidance for three states. | Not planned |
| Existing coverage | State statutes (`STATE`): Pennsylvania | Unconsolidated statutes beyond 35 P.S. ch. 780. | Planned |
| Point-in-time federal law | List of Sections Affected (LSA), monthly, 1997 to 2026 | A month-by-month map from a CFR section to the change that affected it. | Planned |
| Point-in-time federal law | FAR and DFARS class deviation text | Model deviation text that 48 C.F.R. does not yet reflect. | Planned |
| Federal court rules | Federal local rules | District court local rules and local bankruptcy rules not yet held. | Planned |
| Tax | Chief Counsel Advice | Written determinations issued by IRS Chief Counsel to field offices. | Planned |
| Tax | Private Letter Rulings | Taxpayer-specific rulings on a proposed transaction. | Planned |
| Administrative adjudication | DOL OALJ, ARB and BRB | Decisions of the Office of Administrative Law Judges, the Administrative Review Board and the Benefits Review Board. | Planned |
| Administrative adjudication | FDIC, OCC and Federal Reserve | Enforcement actions and consent orders. | Planned |
| Administrative adjudication | SEC ALJ initial decisions | A separate series from the Commission opinions already served. | Planned |
| Administrative adjudication | OSHRC, FMSHRC, FERC, CFTC reparations, US Tax Court, ASBCA and CBCA, EPA Environmental Appeals Board, HHS Departmental Appeals Board | Decisions of each body. | On demand |
| Federal regulatory dockets | regulations.gov | Rulemaking dockets, documents and public comments. | Planned |
| Federal regulatory dockets | FCC ECFS | Telecom proceeding filings. | Planned |
| Federal regulatory dockets | SEC EDGAR comment letters | SEC staff comment letters (`UPLOAD`) and filer correspondence (`CORRESP`). | Planned |
| Agencies not yet covered | Customs and Border Protection (CBP) | Import classification, valuation and admissibility. | Planned |
| Agencies not yet covered | Food and Drug Administration (FDA) | Guidance documents and warning letters. | Planned |
| Agencies not yet covered | Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) | Fair housing guidance. | Planned |
| Agencies not yet covered | Department of Justice (DOJ) Civil Rights Division | Americans with Disabilities Act guidance. | Planned |
| Pending federal legislation | Congressional bills (`corpusType=FEDERAL_BILL`) | Bill text is available for the 119th Congress. Status (actions, sponsors, committees) is still to come. | Text available, status planned |
| Case law pagination | United States Reports | Official star pagination for Supreme Court opinions, so a pin cite such as `576 U.S. 644, 681` is preserved. | Evaluating |

Per-source detail for agencies already covered is in [Agency guidance coverage](/api-guide/coverage/agency-guidance). Adjudication bodies already served are in [Federal coverage](/api-guide/coverage/federal).

`FEDERAL_BILL` text is labelled proposed and is excluded from unscoped searches, browse and totals. Enacted text is served under `corpusType=SESSION_LAW` and, once codified, `corpusType=USC`.

## Out of scope

| Not building | Why |
|---|---|
| General case law and court dockets | The corpus is primary law: statutes, regulations, constitutions, court rules and agency material. |
| GAO and Comptroller General decisions | GAO offers no feed or API and does not permit automated access. |
| Judge-specific procedural material (standing orders, chambers procedures, individual practice rules) | It has no stable location, citation form or amendment cycle. |
| Practice notes and practice directions | A Commonwealth instrument with no direct US federal equivalent. |
| Municipal and county ordinances | No comprehensive public source. |
| Standards incorporated by reference into the CFR (ASTM, NFPA, ISO) | Published by private standards bodies. |
| NAIC model law text | A commercially sold publication, not government primary law. Each state's insurance bulletins are in scope under `STATE_AGENCY_GUIDANCE`. |

The corpus carries court rules that a court formally adopts as binding on everyone appearing before it. That covers the Federal Rules, Supreme Court Rules, the state court rules in the [Coverage](/api-guide/coverage) matrix and the federal local rules.
