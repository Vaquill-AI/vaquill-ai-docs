**`400`** `idempotency-key-too-long`

The `Idempotency-Key` you sent is longer than the API will store. The `detail` names both the maximum and the length we received.

## What to do

Generate a shorter key. A UUID is well inside the limit and is what most integrations use. Do not trim an existing key to fit: two long keys that differ only past the cut point would collapse onto one value, and your retry would come back holding somebody else's operation. Mint a fresh short key per logical launch instead.
