**`502`** `web-research-unavailable`

The upstream web-research provider failed, refused the request, or could not read the page you asked for.

**A `502` rather than a `500`**, and the distinction is the point: nothing of ours is broken. A `500` means open a ticket; this means try again.

The provider's own error message is deliberately not forwarded. It names a vendor this API does not publish, and on some failures it echoes the shape of the request we sent.

## What to do

Retry. Nothing was stored and nothing was charged.

If it persists on one specific URL rather than on every call, the page itself is the problem: it may be unreachable, behind a login, or refusing automated fetching. Other URLs will keep working.
