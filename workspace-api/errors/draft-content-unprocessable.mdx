**`422`** `draft-content-unprocessable`

The markdown you sent passed the schema and could not be turned into a draft body. The field was a valid string; the conversion of it is what failed.

## What to do

Resend simpler markdown. Headings, paragraphs and lists convert cleanly. Tables and fenced code blocks are not part of a draft and are the usual cause when a body that looks fine is refused. Empty or whitespace-only content lands here too, because writing it would replace a customer's contract with a blank page and report success.

<Note>
Do not go hunting for a malformed field. This slug exists precisely to tell you the field name was right and the content was the problem.
</Note>
