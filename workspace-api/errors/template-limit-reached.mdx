**`409`** `template-limit-reached`

Creating another template would cross one of two ceilings your organization shares with the web app:

- **200 active templates per organization.** Trashed and archived templates do not count.
- **50 template creates per day**, counted against the person your installation is attributed to.

`detail` says which one was hit, and the problem carries `limit` plus either `active` or `createdToday` so you do not have to discover the number by trying.

## Why this is a 409 and not a 429

A `429` promises that waiting will help. The per-organization cap never clears on its own: it needs a template deleted. The daily cap does roll, after 24 hours, but the two share an error so that an integration handles the one condition, reads `detail`, and does the right thing rather than retrying into a wall.

## What these caps are, and what they are not

They are not metering and not a usage quota. This API does not meter anything. They exist because template counts are shared with the product: an integration that creates fifty templates in a day silently blocks the next browser upload by the person your installation runs as, with a message about a daily limit they did not spend. Refusing here is refusing to spend somebody else's allowance.

## What to do

Delete a template you no longer run, then retry. `DELETE /v1/templates/{templateId}` retires one immediately and past template runs survive it.

If you are importing a library in bulk and expect to cross 200 legitimately, ask us to lift it rather than working around it.
