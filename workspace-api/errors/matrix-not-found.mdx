**`404`** `matrix-not-found`

No matrix with that id is readable under the matter in the path. Another organization's matrix, one under a different matter of your own, and a malformed id are one answer, so no caller can probe for matrices it cannot already see.

## What to do

List the matrices on the matter you meant and take the id from the response. Matrix ids look like `mtx_` plus 32 hex characters. Row, column and cell ids are separate namespaces (`row_`, `col_`, `cel_`) and none of them can stand in for a matrix id.

Full error reference: [Errors](/workspace-api/concepts/errors)
