**`404`** `message-not-found`

No turn with that id is readable under the matter in the path.

A message id is globally unique, but the route names a MATTER, and the message's own conversation has to be in it. A message from a conversation in another of your matters answers this rather than resolving, which is deliberate: widening silently to the organization would cross the fence an allowlisted installation exists to hold.

## What to do

Read the conversation's turns with `GET /v1/matters/{matterId}/chats/{chatId}/messages` and take the id from there, or take it from the `resource.id` on the operation that produced the answer.

Full error reference: [Errors](/workspace-api/concepts/errors)
