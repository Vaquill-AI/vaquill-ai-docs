**`500`** `internal-error`

Something failed on our side and we do not know what. This is the one error in the taxonomy that carries no diagnosis, deliberately: the original exception text is never put on the wire, because it would describe our internals to a caller who cannot act on it and could not be withdrawn once shipped.

Your request did not complete. Whether anything was written before the failure is not knowable from this response.

## What to do

Retry with backoff. If the call was a launch, send the **same** `Idempotency-Key` you used the first time, which is exactly the case that header exists for: if the operation was in fact created before the failure, you get it back rather than a second billed job.

If it keeps happening, contact support and quote the `requestId` from the response body, or the `X-Request-ID` header. It is logged against your call on our side, along with the exception, so it turns the conversation into a lookup.

<Note>
An unhandled failure raises an alert on our side the moment it happens. You are not the only one who knows.
</Note>
