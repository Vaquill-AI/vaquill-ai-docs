**`503`** `credential-store-unavailable`

We could not verify your credential because a dependency of ours is down. The request was refused rather than allowed through unauthenticated.

## What to do

Retry with exponential backoff. There is nothing to fix on your side.

<Warning>
This is deliberately not a `401`. Telling you your credential was bad during our own outage would send you off rotating a credential that was never the problem, and it would hide the incident from both of us. If you see this, do not touch your credentials: check our status and retry.
</Warning>
