**`422`** `research-scope-rejected`

`groundIn` and `alsoConsider` were both sent, and they mean different things.

- **`groundIn` LOCKS** the answer to the documents you name. The legal corpus and the web are suppressed, and the answer is grounded only in those files.
- **`alsoConsider` ADDS** the documents to what is searched. The corpus and the web stay in play.

Internally the two resolve as "the locked list, or else the additive one", so a request carrying both would silently drop `alsoConsider` and answer as though you had only sent `groundIn`. Refusing is the only way you find out.

## What to do

Send one of them. Use `groundIn` when the documents ARE the answer (what does this contract say), and `alsoConsider` when they are context for a wider question (how does this contract compare to the market).
