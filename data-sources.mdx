## What Vaquill adds on top

- AI question-answering grounded in retrieved opinions and statutes, with claim-level verification.
- A citation graph with treatment classification (followed, distinguished, criticized, overruled) and top-citer aggregation.
- Cross-corpus retrieval that combines case law with statutes, regulations, and state codes in a single query.
- Boolean and natural-language search tuned for legal phrasing, with Bluebook-style citation parsing and normalization.
