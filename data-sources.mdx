Vaquill is built on a broad corpus of authoritative US legal data, layered with Vaquill's own indexing, ranking, and AI pipeline. This page gives a high-level view of what's covered so you know exactly what you can search, cite, and build on.

## US statutes and regulations (API and MCP)

The public REST API and the Vaquill MCP server are statutes-only. They cover the US Code, the CFR, all 50 state statute codes, the U.S. Constitution, the Federal Rules, and Executive Orders: 1.6M+ statute sections in total (273,787 federal sections plus ~1.3M state sections), 2024 edition, refreshed monthly.

## What Vaquill adds on top

- In the app: AI question-answering grounded in retrieved opinions and statutes, with claim-level verification.
- In the app: a citation graph with treatment classification (followed, distinguished, criticized, overruled) and top-citer aggregation.
- In the app: cross-corpus retrieval that combines case law with statutes, regulations, and state codes in a single query.
- Boolean and natural-language search tuned for legal phrasing, with Bluebook-style citation parsing and normalization.
