Vaquill is sold as a single subscription at **$99 / month** that includes the full feature set - every legal tool, AI research mode, citation tooling, and matter workspace. There are no feature gates between tiers, no separate seat-based pricing, and no upcharge for advanced tools like Deep Research or Document Matrix.

Every new account starts with a 7-day free trial that gives you the same experience as a paid subscriber. After the trial, continue at $99 / month or cancel - no charge if you cancel before the trial ends.

For enterprise contracts, multi-seat firm pricing, or custom data residency arrangements, contact [sales](mailto:contact@vaquill.ai).

<Check>
  Pricing is shown in USD. The same $99 / month subscription unlocks every feature; there are no per-feature add-ons.
</Check>

## API Access

The statutes API is sold separately from the platform subscription. It covers the US Code, the CFR, and all 50 state statute codes. See [Credits](/credits) for API credit pricing and the [API Reference](/api-reference/statutes/search-us-statutes) for endpoints.

## Billing

- Subscriptions renew monthly
- Cancel at any time from your dashboard; access continues through the end of the current billing period
- Invoices and receipts are available in your settings

## Billing FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What happens when I cancel?">
    Your access continues through the end of the current billing period. There is no proration of the unused time and no auto-renewal once you cancel.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if I cancel during the free trial?">
    No charge. If you cancel before the 7-day trial ends, the card on file is never billed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I switch from a personal subscription to a firm-wide plan?">
    Yes. Contact [sales](mailto:contact@vaquill.ai) and they will pro-rate the personal subscription against the firm-wide plan so you do not pay twice.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I find invoices and receipts?">
    Open your dashboard, go to **Settings -> Billing**, and download any invoice from the history list.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is the API included in the subscription?">
    No. The statutes API (US Code, CFR, and all 50 state statute codes) is sold separately and metered in API credits. See [Credits](/credits) for pricing.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Tips

<Tip>
  **Use the 7-day trial fully.** You get the same features as paid subscribers; try the high-value tools (Contract Review, Compare Documents, Deep Research) before deciding.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  **API and platform are separate.** You can subscribe to one without the other depending on whether you need the UI, the API, or both.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  **For firm-wide deployments, contact sales first.** Multi-seat arrangements are cheaper than stacking individual subscriptions.
</Tip>
