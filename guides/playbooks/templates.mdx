Most teams should not start a playbook from a blank page.
The template gallery ships 28 curated starting points that already carry real negotiation depth: an ordered fallback ladder per clause, a walk-away floor, a negotiability tier, a sign-off level, and in most cases conditional escalation rules.
Cloning one gives you an editable playbook of your own in a single click.

<Info>
  Templates come in pairs, one tuned for each side of the deal.
  A vendor and a customer want inverted positions on liability, indemnification, and IP, so "SaaS, Customer side" and "SaaS, Vendor side" are genuinely different playbooks rather than the same file with the labels swapped.
</Info>

## The gallery

Open **Start from a template** on the Playbooks page or on the new-playbook form.
Templates are grouped into five sections, and you can search across name, use case, contract type, side, and the clause names each template tunes.

### Commercial

| Template | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| **SaaS - Customer side** | Buying SaaS. Tightens liability cap protections, secures IP and data-breach indemnities, locks down auto-renewal and data-portability rights, and demands proper sub-processor controls. |
| **SaaS - Vendor side** | Selling SaaS. Caps liability proportionally, scopes indemnification to IP infringement, secures payment discipline, and protects feedback and usage-data rights. |
| **MSA - Customer side** | A master services framework you sign once and order against. Tighter warranties, IP-favorable on deliverables, balanced liability, termination flexibility. |
| **MSA - Vendor side** | Vendor-side master agreement. Tight liability cap, IP retained, payment discipline, reasonable termination protections. |
| **Professional Services (PSA + SOW) - Client side** | Own deliverables as created, a real acceptance and rejection right, and an order of precedence where the statement of work controls. |
| **Professional Services (PSA + SOW) - Provider side** | Assigns deliverables on payment, keeps a deemed-acceptance mechanic, retains tools and methodologies, caps liability per statement of work. |
| **Consulting / Professional Services - Client side** | Hiring a consultant or services firm. Strong work-product IP transfer, milestone-tied payments, named-personnel commitments, balanced termination. |
| **Consulting / Professional Services - Provider side** | The consulting firm being engaged. |
| **License Agreement - Licensee side** | Licensing software, data, or IP in. Locks down scope of use, audit limitations, IP indemnification, and fair use after termination. |
| **License Agreement - Licensor side** | Licensing out. Narrow grant, all rights reserved, audit and true-up rights preserved, capped IP indemnity. |
| **Reseller / Channel - Supplier side** | Appointing a reseller. Narrow revocable brand license, pricing floors, end-user terms flowed through, liability for the reseller's conduct limited. |
| **Reseller / Channel - Reseller side** | Workable margins and price protection, a real supplier IP and product indemnity, deal registration and non-circumvention, post-termination pipeline commissions. |
| **Service Level Agreement (SLA) - Customer side** | Negotiating uptime and support commitments as the buyer. |
| **Service Level Agreement (SLA) - Vendor side** | Committing to uptime. Achievable target, realistic exclusions, credits as the sole financial remedy, bounded downtime liability. |
| **AI / Gen-AI Addendum - Customer side** | Buying AI features on top of a SaaS or services deal. Secures output ownership, a strict no-training default, an output-infringement indemnity, and a real service warranty despite the hallucination disclaimer. |
| **AI / Gen-AI Addendum - Vendor side** | Providing AI features. |
| **M&A Acquisition - Buyer side** | Acquiring a private company by stock, asset, or merger. |
| **M&A Acquisition - Seller side** | Selling a private company or its assets. Qualifies reps and narrows the indemnity exposure. |

### Privacy and Data

| Template | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| **DPA - Controller side** | Pushing personal data to a vendor and setting the processing terms. |
| **DPA - Processor side** | The vendor processing customer data. Realistic breach response windows, sub-processor notice patterns, liability limited outside vendor negligence. |
| **HIPAA BAA - Covered Entity side** | The covered entity, or an upstream business associate, sharing protected health information. |
| **HIPAA BAA - Business Associate side** | The vendor handling protected health information. Commits to the mandatory obligations while keeping breach windows operationally workable and capping liability. |

### Non-Disclosure

| Template | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| **Mutual NDA** | Two-way confidentiality for evaluation, diligence, or pre-contract collaboration. Balanced obligations with carve-outs for compelled disclosure and independently developed information. |
| **One-way NDA - Receiving party** | You are the one being asked to sign and take on the obligations. |

### HR and Employment

| Template | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| **Employment - Employer side** | Offer letters and employment contracts. Reasonable confidentiality and IP assignment, jurisdiction-aware non-compete handling, at-will or notice-based termination. |
| **Employment - Employee side** | Reviewing an offer. Narrows IP assignment to work within scope, rejects non-competes where they are void, tightens "for cause", and preserves whistleblower and regulator-reporting rights. |

### Intellectual Property

| Template | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| **IP Assignment - Assignee side** | Receiving IP from a contractor, founder, or acquired company. Full assignment of work product, trailing IP delivery, moral-rights waiver where lawful, warranties against third-party encumbrances. |
| **IP Assignment - Assignor side** | The party assigning IP out. |

## Preview before you clone

Every card has two actions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Preview">
    Opens the full position list for that template: preferred position, acceptable range, fallback ladder shown best to worst, walk-away floor, escalation triggers, and rationale for each clause.
    Clauses tagged as must-have carry a badge, as do clauses that require sign-off, and conditional rules are spelled out in plain language such as "If deal value over 1000000, require GC sign-off".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use this template">
    Clones the template into a new playbook you own and opens it in the editor.
    Nothing is shared or linked back: it is your copy from that moment on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Positions are resolved for your active jurisdiction when the template is cloned.
  A few clauses, non-competes in particular, carry jurisdiction-specific wording, so the copy you get reflects the jurisdiction that was selected at clone time rather than a generic one.
</Note>

## After cloning

Treat the clone as a first draft of your house position, not a finished one.

- Read the walk-away floors first. Those are the strongest statements in the file and the ones most likely to be wrong for your business.
- Check the sign-off levels against how your team actually escalates.
- Run the [Structure Check](/guides/playbooks/structure-check) to see the score the template starts you at.
- Compare it against the market-standard baseline from inside the editor to see where the template is harder or softer than typical market terms.

## Limitations

- Templates are curated starting points, not legal advice, and not tailored to your business. Review before you rely on one.
- A clone is a snapshot. Improvements made to a template later do not flow into playbooks you already created from it.
- Coverage is 14 agreement types. Anything outside that list starts from a blank playbook or from an [imported contract](/guides/playbooks/import-from-contracts).
- Depth varies by template. Most carry conditional escalation rules; the M&A pair does not.
- The market-standard comparison panel is hidden for M&A and for the specialized addenda, because there is no meaningful generic baseline to compare those against.
- The gallery does not merge templates. If you need both a services framework and a data processing addendum, that is two playbooks.
