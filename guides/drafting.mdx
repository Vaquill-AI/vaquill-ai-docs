Drafting turns a plain-English description into a legal document. Tell it what you need, pick a quick-start category or write a custom prompt, set the jurisdiction, and you get a draft you can refine in place. Every draft is saved, organized into folders, and exportable.

## Describe what you need

The prompt box is the front door. Describe your document in plain English and include the details that matter:

- Parties and their roles
- Key terms, amounts, and dates
- Jurisdiction
- Any specific clauses you need (or want left out)

You do not need to know the right legal phrasing. *"A mutual NDA between Acme Corp and a freelance designer, two-year term, governed by California law, with a carve-out for independently developed work"* is enough to produce a usable first draft.

## Choose a jurisdiction

Drafting is jurisdiction-aware. The jurisdiction selector (for example, California) sets the legal context for the draft, so statutory references, required notices, and default positions match the state you select. Set it before you generate so the first draft is already aimed at the right venue.

<Note>
  Vaquill is a US product. The jurisdiction selector covers US federal and all 50 state jurisdictions, backed by the US Code, CFR, and 50-state statute codes.
</Note>

## New draft vs Improve

The prompt box has two modes:

- **New draft** generates a fresh document from your description.
- **Improve** takes an existing draft (or pasted text) and revises it. Use it to tighten language, add a missing clause, change a party, or adapt a document to a different jurisdiction.

Switch modes with the toggle next to the prompt box. Improve keeps the document you are working on and applies your instruction as an edit rather than starting over.

## Drafts and folders

Every draft you generate is saved automatically and listed in the **Drafts** panel. Drafts carry a status (for example, a custom draft or an imported one) and a last-edited time, and you can organize them into folders to keep matters separate.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Generate or import">
    Create a draft from a prompt, or import an existing document to revise.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refine in Improve mode">
    Iterate on the draft until the language is right. Each change is saved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="File it in a folder">
    Move the draft into a folder so it sits with the rest of the matter's work.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pick up where you left off

The start screen surfaces a **Pick up where you left off** row with your most recently edited drafts, each showing when it was last touched. Click one to jump straight back into it, or open **View all drafts** for the full list. You never lose a draft to a closed tab.

## Exporting

When a draft is ready, export it for use outside Vaquill. Drafts export to Word (DOCX) so you can hand them to a counterparty, drop them into a matter, or take them through a final human review before signature.

<Tip>
  Pair drafting with a [Playbook](/guides/playbooks) to bake your firm's preferred clause language and fallback positions into every draft, then score the result section by section before you export.
</Tip>
