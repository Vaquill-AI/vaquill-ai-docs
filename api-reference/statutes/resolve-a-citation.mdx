---
openapi: GET /api/v1/statutes/resolve
description: "Resolve a Bluebook citation string directly to its section - the one-call way to verify a citation an LLM produced."
---

Pass a citation string (`42 U.S.C. § 1983`, `16 C.F.R. § 444.1`, `Del. Code
Ann. tit. 13, § 1301`) as `cite` and get back the exact section, confirmed. A
pinpoint subsection resolves to its parent section, echoed back in
`subsection`. `resolved: false` on a citation that does not match anything -
that is a genuine, useful answer when you're checking whether a citation is
real, not an error. See
[Resolve and verify a citation](/api-guide/recipes/citation-lookup).
