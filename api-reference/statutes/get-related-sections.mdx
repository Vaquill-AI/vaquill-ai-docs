---
openapi: GET /api/v1/us/statutes/section/{actId}/related
description: "Fetch the immediate statutory neighbors of a section - the previous and next section within the same chapter, part, or code."
---

Pass the `actId` of a section you already have to get its neighbors within
the same container: what a lawyer would actually turn to next when reading a
section in context, not simple numeric adjacency. Returns `previous` and
`next` arrays (full section objects) plus a `container` label.
