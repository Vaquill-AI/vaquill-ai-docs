---
openapi: GET /api/v1/statutes/laws
description: "Catalog of every distinct body of law available - a state's statutes, a state's regulations, the US Code, and so on - each with a stable key and section count. Free."
---

Each entry carries a stable `key`, its `corpusType` (pass straight to
`/statutes/search`), a `lawType` (`statutes`, `regulations`, `constitution`,
`court rules`, ...), a human-readable `name`, and a `sectionCount`. Pass
`jurisdiction` (a state code, or `federal`) to scope the list. Use it to
build a picker of what's queryable, or to ground an LLM in the available
bodies of law. Free - no credits charged.
