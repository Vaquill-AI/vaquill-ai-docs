The SDKs retry `408`, `429` and `5xx` responses. They retry a `409` only for `idempotency_in_progress`.
Connection errors and timeouts are retried when it is safe to do so.
The default is 2 retries. Set the retry limit to 0 to turn retries off.
Backoff starts at 0.5 seconds and doubles up to 8 seconds, with up to 25 percent jitter.
The wait comes from the `retry-after-ms` header first, then `Retry-After`, then the body's `retryAfterSeconds`.
A wait above 120 seconds is not slept. The error is raised at once.
Never retried: a `402` `insufficient_credits`, `daily_cap_exceeded`, any other `4xx`, and anything the server marks `retryable: false`.
