A `429` or `503` that sends a `Retry-After` header also carries `retryAfterSeconds` in the error body.
Both are whole seconds, and for a rate limit they count down to the end of the window that refused you.
Wait that long, then retry the same request.
If there is no hint, retry with exponential backoff.
`daily_cap_exceeded` is also a `429`, but retrying cannot succeed until the cap resets.
