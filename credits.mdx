Vaquill bills the API in **credits**. One credit = **$0.01 USD**. Every endpoint has a fixed credit cost - per call, regardless of result size - and your balance updates in real time on the [dashboard](https://app.vaquill.ai/settings).

<Info>
  **New accounts start with 500 free credits ($5).** No credit card required. Sign up at [app.vaquill.ai](https://app.vaquill.ai).
</Info>

## Costs by endpoint

Grouped by capability so you can model your application's per-query cost before you build.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Ask (legal Q&A)" icon="comment-question">
    The flagship endpoint. Routes a question through the full retrieval and generation pipeline and returns a grounded answer with cited sources.

    | Endpoint | Method | Credits | USD |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | `/api/v1/ask` | POST | 15 | $0.15 |
    | `/api/v1/ask` (deep mode) | POST | 30 | $0.30 |
    | `/api/v1/ask/stream` | POST | 15 / 30 | Same as sync |

    Use `standard` mode for fast single-hop answers; switch to `deep` for multi-hop reasoning across sources.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Research and search" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Boolean and natural-language search over the legal corpus.

    | Endpoint | Method | Credits | USD |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | `/api/v1/research/search` (1-20 results) | POST | 1 | $0.01 |
    | `/api/v1/research/search` (21-50 results) | POST | 2 | $0.02 |
    | `/api/v1/research/search` (51-100 results) | POST | 3 | $0.03 |
    | `/api/v1/research/quick` | POST | 1 | $0.01 |

    The quick endpoint returns the top 3-5 results with essentials only.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Citations" icon="quote-left">
    Resolve, look up, search, and traverse the citation network.

    | Endpoint | Method | Credits | USD |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | `/api/v1/citations/resolve` | GET | 1 | $0.01 |
    | `/api/v1/citations/cases/search` | GET | 1 | $0.01 |
    | `/api/v1/citations/cases/lookup` | GET | 1 | $0.01 |
    | `/api/v1/citations/cases/network` | GET | 5 | $0.05 |

    Network traversal is the priciest because it walks multiple hops in the citation graph.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="US statutes (USC + CFR)" icon="landmark">
    Semantic search over the United States Code and Code of Federal Regulations.

    | Endpoint | Method | Credits | USD |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | `/api/v1/statutes/search` | POST | 4 | $0.04 |
    | `/api/v1/statutes/section` | GET | 2 | $0.02 |
    | `/api/v1/statutes/section/body` | GET | 6 | $0.06 |

    Section metadata is cheaper than the full section body.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  **Optimize cost by choosing the right endpoint.** For a hit-or-miss query, start with `/research/quick` ($0.01) before falling back to `/ask` ($0.15). For citation resolution, the resolve endpoint is 15x cheaper than asking the LLM to do it.
</Tip>

## Checking your balance

View remaining credits in your [developer settings](https://app.vaquill.ai/developer?tab=credits). When credits reach zero, API calls return `403`.

<Warning>
  Out-of-credit calls return **`403 Forbidden`**, not `402`. Handle both in your error logic, or top up before you hit zero with auto-recharge in your dashboard.
</Warning>
